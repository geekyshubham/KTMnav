KTM Nav (KTMnav) Privacy Policy
Effective date: 26 October 2026
Developer: Shubham Takankhar
Contact: takankhar@gmail.com

KTM Nav is an independent Android companion for compatible motorcycle dashboards. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, hosted backend, or automatic upload of your data to the developer. The app does not request Internet access. Processing happens on your phone, and enabled dashboard features send the necessary information over Bluetooth to the motorcycle you connect.

Navigation, notifications, and media
With Android notification access, the app reads navigation notifications from supported apps to extract turn guidance, road names, distances, and arrival information. It can read media notifications to show the current track and operate playback. Optional message mirroring reads sender names and message text from the messaging apps you choose and sends a short banner to your connected dashboard. Message text is not saved to disk or included in diagnostic logs. People who can see your dashboard may see these banners. You can disable mirroring, hide message text, choose apps, or mute banners in Settings.

Location and rides
Optional ride recording, speed alerts, and waypoints use your phone's location and speed. Recording saves location coordinates, times, and ride statistics as GPX files in app-private storage. When these features run while the app is not visible, an Android foreground service keeps the activity visible through a notification. Saved rides remain on your phone until you delete them or clear app storage. Exporting or sharing a ride sends it only to the app or recipient you choose.

Calls
If you enable call features and grant the related Android permissions, the app observes incoming call state, can display caller information available in notifications, and lets mapped handlebar buttons answer or silence a ringing call. It does not record call audio or upload call history. KTM Nav does not request access to your contacts database or SMS inbox.

Bluetooth and motorcycle data
Nearby-device access finds and connects to a motorcycle dashboard or an optional ELM327 OBD-II adapter. Saved bike names, device identifiers, pairing information, settings, and supported dashboard values are stored on your phone. Read-only OBD features display data received from your adapter. Data sent to a connected dashboard can include navigation, media, caller, or message information according to the features you enable.

Accessibility service
Optional handlebar screen controls use Android's AccessibilityService API to inspect controls on the current screen and perform scrolling, focus, selection, long-press, and Back actions in response to your mapped physical button presses. Screen content is processed temporarily on your phone, is not recorded, and is not sent to the developer. This app is not a disability accessibility tool. You must enable the service yourself after the in-app disclosure. You can revoke it in Android Accessibility settings at any time.

Local files and diagnostics
Preferences, saved bikes, ride files, and diagnostics are stored locally. Optional maneuver capture saves navigation-arrow images locally for troubleshooting. Sharing diagnostics, maneuver captures, or rides is initiated by you using Android's share screen; the destination's privacy policy then applies. Review exported files before sharing because diagnostics and ride files can contain device or trip information. The app does not sell personal data or automatically send it to advertisers or other third parties.

Retention and deletion
Delete saved bikes and rides in the app. Clear app storage or uninstall KTM Nav to remove its app-private files and preferences. Copies you exported to other apps or recipients must be deleted separately. Revoke notification, location, phone, nearby-device, and Accessibility access through Android settings. No developer-held account or cloud data exists to delete.

Other apps and support
Navigation, messaging, media, and other apps you use have their own privacy policies. If you email support, your email address and the information you include are used to respond to your request and retained only as needed for that correspondence. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Audience and changes
KTM Nav is intended for motorcycle riders and is not directed at children. Updates to data handling will be reflected in this policy. Contact takankhar@gmail.com with privacy questions.
